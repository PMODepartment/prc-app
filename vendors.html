Add a vendor to the directory — fill in what you know now, invite them to self-manage their profile later if needed.
Merge Vendors
Fold duplicate records (typos / different names for one company) into a single canonical vendor. All their products, certifications, personnel, rates, bids and linked work packages move to the one you keep; the duplicates are then deleted. Garbled entries that look like SEVERAL company names joined together are handled by the separate Split tool instead — they never appear here as a suggested duplicate.
Suggested Duplicate Groups
Auto-detected by comparing vendor names — review each group, then merge or uncheck any entry that doesn't actually belong. "Merge All Groups" processes every group at once using whatever canonical/checked selections are currently shown.

Remove Exact Duplicates
Vendors whose names are identical after ignoring case and extra spaces. One record is kept per name and the rest are folded into it — products, certifications, personnel, rates, bid history, work-package links and trade categories all move to the record that is kept, so nothing is lost.

How to use this grid — works like Excel
Click select · drag select a range · Enter/F2 or just type to edit
Tab/arrows move · Shift+arrows extend selection · Ctrl+arrows jump to edge of data · Ctrl+Shift+arrows extend to edge of data · Del clear · Ctrl+Z undo · Esc cancel
Ctrl+C/V copy & paste — multi-cell, round-trips with Excel. Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C/V copies whole WPs and pastes them as new rows (works across projects).
Ctrl+D or drag the corner handle to fill down · header ▾ sets a whole column at once
Header 📌 freezes columns up to that point (click it again to unfreeze) · your choice is remembered
Drag a column border to resize (double-click to reset). Edits are staged until you press Save.
Add WP adds the number of blank rows in the box beside it (up to 100) at once — then Tab/Enter walks you straight through its required fields, skipping anything already filled in.
Dropdown cells (Trade, Works, Status…) let you type to filter instead of scrolling; pasted or typed text is matched to the real option regardless of case/spacing.
Select all → Copy WPs, then open another project's grid and Paste WPs to bring those work packages in as new rows.

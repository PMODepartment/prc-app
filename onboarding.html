Welcome
Overview

What is the WPM Dashboard?

A single place to track procurement work packages across every Megawide EPC project — their award status, budgets, schedule and contractors — all live from a shared database.

  • One source of truth. Everyone sees the same live data; no more juggling spreadsheet versions.
  • Dashboards + detail. Portfolio-wide KPIs and charts, down to individual work-package records.
  • Role-based. What you see and can change depends on your role — covered in this guide.

Getting started

Signing in & picking where to go

Log in at the sign-in page, then choose your destination. New here? Register first — the PMO / Procurement team approves accounts and sets your role and project access.

  • Sign in with your email & password (reset via “Forgot password”).
  • Portfolio Overview — all projects consolidated (admins & specialists see everything).
  • A specific project — jump straight into one project's dashboard.
Don't see a project you need? Ask the PMO team to add it to your assignments.

Navigation

Two ways to look at the data

Switch any time from the sidebar (the ☰ hamburger opens/collapses it). The top bar carries Refresh, Export PDF, a dark-mode toggle and your profile.

Portfolio Overview

All projects in one consolidated dashboard.

  • 6 tabs: Overview · Backlog · Budget · Schedule · Works · WP List
  • Project filter to include/exclude projects
  • Archived projects start unselected

Project Dashboard

One project, in full detail.

  • 3 tabs: Overview · Backlog · WP List
  • Procurement S-Curve (planned vs actual)
  • Inline Status Tracker for editors

Reading the data

Making sense of the charts

KPI cards and charts summarise cost and progress at a glance. (Viewers see counts, status & schedule — cost figures are hidden.)

  • Cost Overview KPIs — Budget (BCB), Actual Award, Balance to Award, Savings / Loss.
  • Procurement S-Curve — cumulative planned vs actual awarding; the dashed line forecasts the remainder.
  • Budget & Awarded by Period and status donuts — spend and award progress by month/quarter.
  • Top 5 panels — highest value, biggest savings, most overbudget work packages (click to open a WP).
Reading the data
Work packages

Finding work packages fast

The WP List groups records by Project → Trade. Headers are sortable and the list is paginated for speed.

  • Cascading filters — Trade → Works → Vendor narrow each other; plus Status, OSM and free-text search.
  • Click a WP No. to open the slide-in detail panel with every field (and Edit / Delete for editors).
  • Filtered Analysis — a period chart + KPIs you can scope to a From/To month range.

Roles
Administrators

Administrators — the full picture

Super Admin & Admin. The widest access: the entire portfolio plus the tools to manage people and projects.

  • See every project and all cost / budget data.
  • Add, edit, delete any work package — saved immediately.
  • Bulk CSV import — de-duplicates by WP No.
  • User Management — approve sign-ups, set roles, assign projects.
  • Project Management — create, edit, archive projects.
  • Review queue for any submissions needing sign-off.
Only a Super Admin can grant the Super Admin or Specialist roles; Admins manage all other roles.
Administrators
Contributors

Contributors — doing the work

Specialist, Manager & User. The day-to-day editors. Full cost visibility, no user/project administration. Changes save immediately (auto-approved).

  • Add / edit / delete work packages on your projects.
  • Import from CSV — re-imports update by WP No. (no duplicates).
  • Status Tracker — update Award / Procurement / Delivery inline.
  • Full cost & budget visibility on all dashboards.
Specialists can view every project (edit their assigned ones). Managers & Users work within their assigned projects.
Contributors
Viewers

Viewers — read-only insight

Viewer. Browse dashboards and work-package details for assigned projects, with financial figures hidden.

  • Browse dashboards, charts and WP detail for your projects.
  • No editing — Add / Edit / Delete and the WP form are unavailable.
  • Cost hidden — budget, awarded amounts and the Budget / Works tabs are not shown.
  • You still see status, schedule, counts and progress everywhere else.
Need to edit or see costs? Ask the PMO team to upgrade your role.

For editors

Adding & updating work packages

Administrators & Contributors keep the data current. Two ways in — the full form or a bulk import.

The WP Form

  • Guided sections: identity, approval, bonds, submittals, schedule, budget, payment, status
  • Trade → Works → Type cascade
  • Safeguards: awarded WPs need cost + vendor; no future award dates

CSV Import

  • Download the template, fill, import
  • De-duplicates by WP No. — existing rows update in place
  • Whole batch validated before anything is saved
For quick status changes, use the Status Tracker on the WP List — no need to open the form.

Handy to know

Tips & getting help

  • Refresh pulls the latest data without a full reload.
  • Dark mode — toggle in the top bar; your choice is remembered.
  • Export PDF — a print-ready table of the current view (editors & admins).
  • Add to Home Screen to install it like an app on your phone.
  • In-app guide — the ? button in the top bar reopens the tour any time.
Questions or access issues? Contact the PMO / Procurement team to update your role or project assignments.
